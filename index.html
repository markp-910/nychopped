Our Story

We went to New York.
We came back obsessed.

It started as a family trip. Tony jumped in at the last minute, because why not fly to New York on a whim to be with friends and family? Somewhere along the way we walked into a little shop in Greenwich Village and ordered a chopped cheese.

“Wow, this is the best sandwich I've ever had. Detroit needs this ASAP.”

That was the whole meeting. The decision was made before the sandwich was gone.

We're starting with a food truck so we can find the best spots, meet the people and build a following one sandwich at a time.

How we do it

Elevated, not expensive.

Michigan sourced

We're working with Michigan suppliers for our beef, bread and produce, so it tastes like New York and helps out folks close to home.

No seed oils

Nothing on our menu is cooked in seed oils. Just better ingredients cooked the right way.

Fair prices

Better food shouldn't cost an arm and a leg. A great sandwich should be an everyday thing.
